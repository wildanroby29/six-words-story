A complete story in exactly six words, for the [Six Words, One Story](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410) bounty on make.fun.

## The story

> The ring fit. The marriage didn't.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the six-word story to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(the grader's `/output` mount). Nothing else runs. The story is the whole entry.
